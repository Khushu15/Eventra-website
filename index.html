Good to know

Questions, answered.

Where is Eventra taking place?

Eventra takes place at PDSCT, Panna Road, Chhatarpur, Madhya Pradesh.

Can I transfer my pass?

Yes. Passes can be transferred once, up to 48 hours before the event.

Are children allowed?

Guests aged 12 and above are welcome with a responsible adult.
